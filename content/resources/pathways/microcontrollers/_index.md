title: "Microcontrollers"
---

Five starter projects that take you from blinking an LED to saving and charting sensor data, using an Arduino and the Arduino Cloud Editor in your Chrome browser. You don't need any experience. Start with the Start Here page, then do M1 to M5 in order.

- [Start Here](/resources/pathways/microcontrollers/start-here/): what a microcontroller is, your kit, and how to set up.
- [M1 Blink](/resources/pathways/microcontrollers/m1-blink/): upload your first sketch and make an LED blink.
- [M2 Button and LED](/resources/pathways/microcontrollers/m2-button-and-led/): inputs, outputs and the breadboard. Control an LED with a button.
- [M3 Light Dimmer](/resources/pathways/microcontrollers/m3-light-dimmer/): read an analog sensor and watch the numbers.
- [M4 Raw vs Library](/resources/pathways/microcontrollers/m4-raw-vs-library/): see what a digital sensor really sends.
- [M5 Data Logger](/resources/pathways/microcontrollers/m5-data-logger/): save your readings and chart them.
- [Reference](/resources/pathways/microcontrollers/reference/): a cheat sheet to look things up.

The log from M5 can be used in Data Science (DS1) and Synthesize (S5). A box for your Arduino can be designed in Design (D3).
