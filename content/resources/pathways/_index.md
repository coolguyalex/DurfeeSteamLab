Four independent starter pathways of hands-on projects. Each pathway stands on its own, so you can start with whichever one interests you. All of them work in a Chromebook browser.

<div class="cards">
<a class="card link" href="/resources/pathways/microcontrollers/">
<h3>Microcontrollers</h3>
<p class="tag">M1 to M5</p>
<p>Program an Arduino to blink, read sensors and log data. Uses the Arduino Cloud Editor.</p>
</a>
<a class="card link" href="/resources/pathways/data-science/">
<h3>Data Science</h3>
<p class="tag">DS1 to DS4</p>
<p>Load, summarize, smooth and measure data, including sound, with Python. Uses Google Colab.</p>
</a>
<a class="card link" href="/resources/pathways/synthesize/">
<h3>Synthesize</h3>
<p class="tag">S1 to S5</p>
<p>Build a synthesizer out of boxes and wires, from a single tone to filters, echo and melodies played from data. Uses Pure Data.</p>
</a>
<a class="card link" href="/resources/pathways/design/">
<h3>Design</h3>
<p class="tag">D1 to D3</p>
<p>Sketch and model 3D parts, then design an enclosure for an Arduino that can be 3D printed. Uses Onshape.</p>
</a>
</div>

## Bridges

The pathways can connect if you want them to. You never have to cross over.

- A data log from Microcontrollers (M5) can be analyzed in Data Science (DS1) and played as sound in Synthesize (S5).
- Pitch numbers from Data Science (DS3) feed Synthesize (S5).
- A box from Design (D3) holds your Arduino, and its sound can be measured in Data Science (DS4).
