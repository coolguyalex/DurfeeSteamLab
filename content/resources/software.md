Everything here is free and runs in the Chrome browser on a school Chromebook. Nothing needs to be installed.

| Tool | What it's for | Where | Account |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Arduino Cloud Editor** | Writing and uploading Arduino sketches ([Microcontrollers](/resources/pathways/microcontrollers/), M1 to M5) | app.arduino.cc | Your teacher will tell you which one |
| **Google Colab** | Running Python in a notebook ([Data Science](/resources/pathways/data-science/), DS1 to DS4) | colab.research.google.com | Your school Google account |
| **Google Sheets** | Charting your logged data (M5) | sheets.google.com | Your school Google account |
| **Pure Data (Pd)** | Building synthesizers from boxes and wires ([Synthesize](/resources/pathways/synthesize/), S1 to S5) | <span class="todo">[browser version still to be chosen]</span> | None |
| **Onshape** | Designing 3D parts to print ([Design](/resources/pathways/design/), D1 to D3) | onshape.com | Free Education plan. Your teacher will tell you which account |

## What has been tested

Not everything here has been tried on a school Chromebook yet.

- **Python in Data Science:** the code was run and gives the numbers shown. The Colab screens and buttons were not tested.
- **Pure Data:** the steps were written from memory and have not been tested. Whether Pd runs well in a Chromebook browser is still an open question.
- **Onshape:** menu names and button positions were written from memory and have not been checked against the live screens.

Tell your teacher if something doesn't match what you see.
