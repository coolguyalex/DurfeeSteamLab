Onshape is a free 3D design program that runs in the browser. It is how lab projects get from a sketch to a part that can be 3D printed, such as enclosures, mounts and brackets.

## Get set up

- **Tool:** [onshape.com](https://www.onshape.com/en/education), free on the Education plan.
- **Account:** ask in room 3106 which account to use.
- **Runs:** in the Chrome browser. A mouse is easier than a trackpad.

## Suggested progression

1. **Learn the basics.** *Read and practice* · [Onshape Learning Center](https://learn.onshape.com) - Onshape's own courses and tutorials. Start here.
2. **Lab projects.** *Lab guide* · [Design Lab Guide](/resources/design/lab-guide/) - D1 First Part, D2 Sphere and Shapes, D3 Enclosure for an Arduino.
