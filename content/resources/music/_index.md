The ideas behind sound and music that the [sound tools](/resources/sound/) assume: pitch and frequency, rhythm, the harmonic series, timbre, scales and what "in key" means. None of it needs an instrument.

## Key ideas in plain terms

- **Pitch and frequency:** pitch is how high or low a note sounds. It follows frequency, the number of vibrations per second, in hertz (Hz). The note A above middle C is 440 Hz. Doubling the frequency raises a note by one octave.
- **Rhythm:** the pattern of sounds in time. The beat is the steady pulse, tempo is its speed, and meter is how beats group (for example, in fours).
- **Harmonic series:** a played note is a stack of frequencies. The lowest is the fundamental (f), and above it sit multiples: 2f, 3f, 4f and so on. These are the harmonics.
- **Timbre:** why a flute and a guitar playing the same note sound different. It comes from how strong each harmonic is. A sine wave has only the fundamental. A saw wave has all the harmonics.
- **Scale and key:** a scale is a chosen set of pitches. The major scale follows the step pattern whole, whole, half, whole, whole, whole, half. A key is built on one scale, and notes are "in key" when they belong to that scale.

## Suggested progression

1. **Hear and make it.** *Interactive* · [Learning Music](https://learningmusic.ableton.com) - Ableton. Make beats, then melodies and chords, in the browser. Covers beats and tempo, pitch, keys and scales, chords, basslines, melodies and song structure.
2. **Put names to it.** *Read and practice* · [musictheory.net](https://www.musictheory.net/lessons) - free lessons, exercises and ear trainers on notation, rhythm and meter, scales, key signatures, intervals and chords.
3. **See what sound is made of.** *Interactive* · [Fourier: Making Waves](https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulations/fourier-making-waves) - University of Colorado's PhET simulation. Add harmonics one by one, hear the sound change, and watch the waveform. Runs in the browser.
   *Read* · [Electronic Music Interactive, lesson 11: Harmonics](https://pages.uoregon.edu/emi/11.php) - University of Oregon. The harmonic series and the harmonic spectrum of a waveform.
4. **How synthesizers shape it.** *Interactive* · [Learning Synths](https://learningsynths.ableton.com) - Ableton. Learn the parts of a synth by playing with one in the page.
5. **Go further (optional).** *Read* · [Open Music Theory](https://viva.pressbooks.pub/openmusictheory/) - a free, complete online text, written for undergraduate courses. Use it as a reference, not a starting point.
