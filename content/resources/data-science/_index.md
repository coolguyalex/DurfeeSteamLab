Python in a notebook, for loading, cleaning, charting and listening to data. The lab uses Google Colab, so there is nothing to install. Useful for sensor logs, fair projects and anything with a spreadsheet's worth of numbers.

## Get set up

- **Tool:** [Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com), free, in the Chrome browser.
- **Account:** a school Google account.

## Suggested progression

1. **First notebook.** *Lab guide* · [Data Science Lab Guide](/resources/data-science/lab-guide/) - set up Colab, then DS1 Load and Summarize and DS2 Smooth and Spot Outliers.
2. **Learn the main library.** *Read* · [10 minutes to pandas](https://pandas.pydata.org/docs/user_guide/10min.html) - the official quick tour.
3. **Practice with short exercises.** *Interactive* · [Kaggle Learn](https://www.kaggle.com/learn) - free, short courses in Python and pandas.
4. **Scales and sound.** *Lab guide* · DS3 Scales and DS4 Sound as Data, in the same [lab guide](/resources/data-science/lab-guide/).
5. **Reference.** *Read* · [Python Data Science Handbook](https://jakevdp.github.io/PythonDataScienceHandbook/) - Jake VanderPlas's free book; the chapters on NumPy, pandas and Matplotlib cover most needs.

## Connections to other tools

Logs from [Arduino](/resources/arduino/) (M5) are good data for DS1. Pitch numbers from DS3 feed Pure Data (PD5) in the [sound tools](/resources/sound/). A box from [Design](/resources/design/) (D3) makes sounds you can record and measure in DS4.
