## Paperwork

- Every project needs Forms 1, 1A, 1B and 2A, a research plan, and a risk assessment (Form 3 is recommended for all projects).
- Projects that need pre-approval also need Form 2B (a qualified scientist), plus Forms 4, 5A or 5B, or 6A as they apply.
- Keep a **project notebook**. It is required and judged, and it must log all AI use.
- Don't create your zFairs account until your research plan is approved. You have two weeks from creating it to finish the submission.
- Teams can have up to 3 students. Each student can be on one project.
